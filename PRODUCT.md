# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React + TypeScript (React 19, Vite 8, TypeScript 6, oxlint). Chosen by the user, TypeScript over plain JS.

## Users

Primary: prospective freelance clients — small businesses, professionals, and founders who need a landing page built. They usually arrive from Instagram or word of mouth, scan the page in seconds on a phone or laptop, and decide whether Nicolò looks capable enough to contact. Their job: judge ability and craft quickly enough to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Personal landing page for Nicolò Florean, a developer who builds landing pages. It exists to convert a visitor into a contact. Success is a real inquiry via email, Instagram, or GitHub from someone who understood what he does.

## Positioning

A versatile developer: websites, web apps, and 3D graphics — not a single-trick landing-page factory.

## Operating Context

- Most first visits come from Instagram or a shared link, often on mobile.
- The page is the portfolio piece: its own quality is the argument for hiring him.
- Visitors compare him against agencies and template sellers; the differentiator is craft and range.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page marketing site; no backend, CMS, or e-commerce.
- Only these contact channels exist: email, Instagram, GitHub.
- Must stay fast on mobile, accessible, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- No fabricated claims, testimonials, client logos, metrics, or pricing.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Nicolò Florean.
- Contact: email `06flonico@gmail.com`, Instagram `@69flore._`, GitHub `github.com/flor3xx`.

## Evidence on Hand

- The three confirmed contact links above.
- No testimonials, case studies, project write-ups, or benchmarks are confirmed. Future work must not invent them; ask before adding any claim or third-party brand.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with demonstrated capability, not adjectives.
2. Make contact frictionless and obvious from the first viewport.
3. Show range (websites, web apps, 3D) without becoming a menu of everything.
4. Craft is the pitch: the page must feel better than the alternatives.
5. Never trade speed or accessibility for visual effect.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-reachable contact actions, readable contrast, and a reduced-motion path that keeps content visible.
